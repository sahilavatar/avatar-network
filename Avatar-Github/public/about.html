What it does

Answers from your notes

Questions are answered using the most relevant passages from what you uploaded, with the source shown. When your notes don’t cover a question, the answer is marked as general knowledge.

Private by design

The AI runs inside your browser. Your files, questions and progress are stored only on this device.

Exact calculators

Equations, molar mass, gas laws, Punnett squares and more are solved exactly with worked steps, not guessed by AI.

Avatars, goals and skills

Give each subject its own Avatar and notebook, set goals with deadlines, and add skills like flashcards or exam prep.

How it works

  1. Download the AI model once (about 1.1–1.3 GB). It's cached in your browser, so later visits load it from your device.
  2. Add your class notes, handouts or PDFs to an Avatar's notebook.
  3. Ask anything. Check each answer against the cited passage, and practise with quiz questions.

Models

Avatar Network uses Google's open Gemma models: Gemma 3 1B to write answers and EmbeddingGemma to find the right passages, run with ONNX Runtime in your browser. It works best with WebGPU: Chrome or Edge on a laptop, or Safari 26+ on a Mac; without it, answers are slower. AI answers can contain mistakes, which is why answers drawn from your notes link to their source and general-knowledge answers are labelled as such.

Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use, including the Gemma Prohibited Use Policy. The sample Avatars' notes and practice questions were written for Avatar Network as demo material.
