Privacy

In short: Avatar Network runs its AI inside your browser. Your notes, questions, chats and progress are stored on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Files you upload and the text extracted from them
  • Your questions and the AI's answers
  • Search indexes built from your notes
  • Your practice results, custom avatars and settings

These are kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB and local storage) for this site. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they're not synced between devices or browsers.

What the app downloads

To run, the app fetches files from these services. They receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type, but never your notes or questions.

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its CDN): the AI models, Gemma 3 1B and EmbeddingGemma, about 1.1–1.3 GB, downloaded once and then cached. Gemma is provided under the Gemma Terms of Use.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): the ONNX Runtime engine that runs the models
  • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): the PDF reader used to extract text from uploaded PDFs
  • Google Fonts: the fonts used by the interface

GitHub, which hosts this website on GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs of page requests, such as IP addresses.

Deleting your data

Use Reset in the Notes tab to delete your uploads, progress and custom avatars, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything, including the downloaded models.
